# 六筒、九筒生成提示词

生成方式：内置 image_gen；分别生成两张新图，未调用 CLI/API fallback。

## 六筒

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: premium Chinese/Nanjing mahjong game tile redesign, front-face master concept, SIX DOTS / 六筒.
Primary request: produce exactly ONE refined realistic ivory resin mahjong tile, with exactly SIX recessed concentric circle pips. Orthographic straight-on front view with no yaw, no skew, no angled perspective; tile face ratio width:height 1:1.38. Show the whole tile, centered, with a small margin on a genuinely transparent background. The tile fills about 88% image height. No text, no label, no numerals, no watermark, no surrounding objects.
Pattern: EXACTLY two columns and three rows: GREEN GREEN on top, RED RED in middle, RED RED at bottom. Clearly group the top two green pips separately from the four red pips; top-to-middle gap is slightly larger than middle-to-bottom gap. Every pip same diameter and a true circle. Normalized positions on the flat FRONT FACE (exclude bevel/back): x = 0.29, 0.71; y = 0.23, 0.55, 0.77. Pip outer diameter = 27% of face width; group width about 69%; group bounding height about 74%. Equal top/bottom optical margins and equal left/right margins; do not crowd bottom.
Materials: smooth fine-grained warm ivory melamine/resin, subtle satin ceramic-like polish, soft rounded bevels, believable softly rounded thick tile silhouette, tiny jade-green backing strip visible at bottom only. Pips are shallow engraved and filled with lacquer, flush/inset, NEVER raised discs or stickers. Each pip: a substantial colored outer ring, narrow ivory separator, colored inner ring, small colored center. Crisp even circular grooves, subtle tiny occlusion only inside cuts. Traditional forest green #174C34 and rich vermilion red #B82B25. Ivory #F3F0E5. Fine realistic material, clean premium mass-produced mahjong, not weathered.
Lighting: broad soft upper-left studio light, restrained specular edges, gentle neutral shadows on bevel, no colored glow. Face evenly lit and legible at thumbnail size.
Constraints: exactly six pips. Do not render coins, poker chips, targets, metal, gems, neon, bloom, thick black outlines, black background, excessive 3D protrusion, ornate rosettes, 3-by-2 layout, labels or measurement lines. This is a fresh design, not an edit of any prior image.
